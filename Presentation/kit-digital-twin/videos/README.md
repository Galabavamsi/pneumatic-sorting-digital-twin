# Presentation Videos

These videos were rendered directly from the validated Unity scenes at 1280 × 720 and 24 frames per second. They are H.264 MP4 files with no audio and are suitable for direct insertion into PowerPoint.

| File | Duration | Demonstrated operation |
|---|---:|---|
| `kit-1-sorting-demo.mp4` | 18 s | Automatic feed, material decision, lift/eject routing, gravity release, and repeated sorting cycles |
| `kit-2-stamping-demo.mp4` | 20 s | Metal and plastic routes through the two stamping paths, transfer, eject, and bin discharge |
| `kit-3-vacuum-sorting-demo.mp4` | 22 s | Feed, stamp, vacuum grip, linear pickup transfer, rotary routing, release, and return home |

The clips show representative cycles rather than the complete 16-workpiece batches. Kits 4–5 have assembly imagery only and no simulation videos are claimed.
